# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Executive Sponsors

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors provide strategic direction, organizational support, and escalation authority for projects. They ensure the project remains aligned with business priorities and has the resources needed to succeed.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm strategic goals, desired outcomes, and success measures
- Secure organizational support, funding, and capacity
- Resolve escalated risks, conflicts, and resource constraints
- Sponsor major go/no-go and scope decisions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Receive status, risk, and decision updates from Project Managers
- Align with Product Managers on expected business value and priority trade-offs
- Support Developers and Technical Leads by removing organizational blockers rather than directing implementation

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate business and user needs into clear requirements, process improvements, and acceptance criteria. They maintain traceability from the problem being solved through delivery validation.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit, analyze, and document business requirements
- Map current and future processes and identify gaps
- Define measurable acceptance criteria with stakeholders
- Support impact analysis, scope clarification, and stakeholder validation

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to refine problems, priorities, and outcomes
- Work with Project Managers to identify dependencies, assumptions, and planning impacts
- Collaborate with Developers and Quality Assurance Leads to make requirements testable and unambiguous

---

## Technical Leads and Solution Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Solution Architects guide technical direction, architecture, and engineering decisions. They make technical risks and constraints visible while enabling Developers to deliver maintainable solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Define and communicate solution architecture and technical standards
- Assess feasibility, estimates, dependencies, and non-functional requirements
- Identify and mitigate technical debt, integration risks, and architectural risks
- Facilitate technical decisions and document significant trade-offs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provide Project Managers with technical estimates, dependencies, and risk information
- Work with Product Managers and Business Analysts to balance value, feasibility, and scope
- Guide Developers through design and implementation without replacing their delivery ownership
- Coordinate with Security and Compliance Partners and Operations Owners on readiness requirements

---

## Quality Assurance and Test Leads

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance and Test Leads establish the quality approach and provide evidence that delivered work meets its acceptance criteria and release standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, coverage expectations, and quality gates
- Validate acceptance criteria and coordinate functional, integration, and regression testing
- Track defects, quality risks, and unresolved test findings
- Report release readiness and recommend mitigations when quality standards are not met

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers and Business Analysts to confirm expected behavior and acceptance criteria
- Collaborate with Developers and Technical Leads on testability, defect resolution, and automation
- Provide Project Managers with quality status, risks, and release evidence
- Coordinate with Operations Owners on monitoring, rollback, and production validation needs

---

## Security and Compliance Partners

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Partners identify security, privacy, regulatory, and policy requirements and help the project meet them throughout the delivery lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Identify applicable controls, threats, privacy obligations, and compliance requirements
- Review designs, data handling, access controls, and risk mitigations
- Document exceptions, approvals, and required remediation
- Confirm security and compliance evidence before release

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Advise Technical Leads and Developers early enough to influence architecture and implementation
- Work with Business Analysts and Product Managers to clarify regulatory and privacy needs
- Report control gaps and approval risks to Project Managers for tracking and escalation
- Coordinate with Operations Owners on secure deployment, monitoring, and incident response

---

## Operations and Service Owners

### Role Summary
Operations and Service Owners represent the reliability, supportability, and ongoing ownership of the service after delivery. They ensure that releases can be operated safely and handed over clearly.

### Responsibilities
- Define operational, reliability, monitoring, support, and recovery requirements
- Assess deployment readiness, rollback plans, and service documentation
- Prepare support teams and confirm incident and escalation procedures
- Monitor post-release outcomes and feed operational learnings into improvement work

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Project Managers to plan handover, operational risks, and post-release success measures
- Collaborate with Technical Leads, Developers, and Quality Assurance Leads on observability and release readiness
- Coordinate with Security and Compliance Partners on production controls and incident obligations
- Share operational feedback with Product Managers to inform prioritization and future improvements

---

## Customer and End-User Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer and End-User Representatives provide firsthand context about user needs, usability, adoption barriers, and whether delivered outcomes solve the intended problem.

### Responsibilities
- Describe user workflows, needs, constraints, and pain points
- Validate prototypes, requirements, and delivered behavior
- Participate in demonstrations, acceptance activities, and outcome reviews
- Surface adoption concerns and provide feedback after release

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers and Business Analysts to clarify needs and validate acceptance criteria
- Provide Project Managers with timely feedback that affects scope, risks, or schedule
- Collaborate with Developers and Quality Assurance Leads during demos and acceptance without taking over technical testing responsibilities
- Share outcome and adoption feedback with Executive Sponsors during reviews
